Operational Details

⚙️ Core Business Processes

Detailed breakdown of each primary business process

🛒

Order Management

  • 1. Customer browses menu and adds items to cart
  • 2. System validates restaurant availability and min. order
  • 3. Payment gateway processes the transaction
  • 4. Order pushed to restaurant's display system (KDS)
  • 5. Status updated in real-time via WebSocket
🛵

Delivery Management

  • 1. Algorithm finds nearest available delivery partner
  • 2. Partner receives push notification with order details
  • 3. Partner accepts and navigates to restaurant
  • 4. GPS tracking shared with customer in real-time
  • 5. OTP-based delivery confirmation system
💰

Payment & Refunds

  • 1. Multi-gateway support: UPI, Cards, Wallets, COD
  • 2. Webhook-based payment status confirmation
  • 3. Escrow holds funds until delivery confirmed
  • 4. Auto-refund within 5-7 days on cancellation
  • 5. FoodCoins loyalty points awarded on delivery
📊

Analytics & Monitoring

  • 1. Real-time dashboard for order volume & status
  • 2. SLA alerts if delivery exceeds 45 minutes
  • 3. Restaurant performance scoring & ranking
  • 4. ML-based demand forecasting per zone
  • 5. Automated reports & KPI tracking
